On this page, we plot the different of fuels used to transport goods by each country. Each color represent a country, the x-axis time. On the y-axis, it's expressed the energy used in thousands of ton of oil equivalent (KTOE), an energy unit used to immediatly compare different energy sources with something ore concrete. On the top left, we can select which type of energy source to plot. Note that the y scale changes as well to better show the differences between nation on the same source.
