In this page we have two maps of Europe. The top one shows how much CO2 equivalent tonnes each European Union country produced each year. Clicking on the play button will start an animation showing the evolution in time, from which we can observe how countries are reducing their impact, reflecting the increasing sensibility general population has on this theme.
In this second map, it's reported the CO2 per capita produced. This way, smaller changes in absolute values are more visible than in the map above, better highlighting the decreasing CO2 production trend is more widespread.
